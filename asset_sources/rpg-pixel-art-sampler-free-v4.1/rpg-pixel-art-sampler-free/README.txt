RPG PIXEL ART SAMPLER (FREE)  v4.1
RetroLabStudioCo - https://furkan90x.itch.io/rpg-pixel-art-sampler-free

A free, ready-to-use starter set of hand-coded pixel art for top-down RPGs:
2 fully animated heroes, 2 fully animated monsters, HUD icons and loot icons
that work together - enough to prototype a first fight.
Every art file is an unmodified original file from one of our twelve full packs.

  All PNGs are RGBA, every pixel fully opaque or fully transparent.
  Scale by whole numbers (2x, 3x, 4x) with nearest-neighbour filtering.
  The palette.gpl files belong to the v3 packs; the v4 files use their own full pack's palette.

NAMING
  characters/<hero>/frames/<hero>_<anim>_<dir>_<nn>.png    anim = idle | walk | attack | hurt | death
  characters/<hero>/sheets/<hero>_<anim>_<dir>.png         one row, frames left to right
  monsters/<monster>/frames/<monster>_<anim>_<dir>_<nn>.png anim = idle | move | attack | hurt | death
  monsters/<monster>/sheets/<monster>_<anim>_<dir>.png
  ui_icons/<size>/<icon>.png   item_icons/<size>/<icon>.png   farm_icons/<size>/<icon>.png
  dir = down | left | right | up   (left is the exact mirror of right)
  v4 files keep the exact names of their full pack, so they match it file for file:
    jack_o_lantern_attack_down_<n>.png (n = 0-9)
    paladin_default_<anim>_s_<nn>.png  (s = south; the full pack has 8 directions)
    spell_fx/<effect>/frames/<effect>_<nn>.png, spell_fx/<effect>/sheets/<effect>_strip.png
